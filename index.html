    Questions?

    What is Dear Brain?

    An iPhone app that helps you scroll less and live more. It gives you real things to do and makes it rewarding to stick with them.

    Which apps can I block?

    Any app you pick: TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, games. They stay locked during your routines, and you can set daily open limits for the rest of the day.

    Do I have to delete social media?

    No. Keep your apps. Dear Brain locks them while you do a routine, and you choose how often they open the rest of the day.

    What can I do instead?

    Running, reading, music, cooking, journaling, prayer, studying, tidying up. Start from 49 ready-made routines or make your own.

    What if I miss a day?

    Nothing dramatic. Days you didn't plan a routine never break your streak, and trophies you've earned are yours to keep.

    Are my photos and notes private?

    Yes. Your memories, notes and photos are kept on your iPhone.
